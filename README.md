## What we build 🛠️

**Apps**, built for real bodies at 3 a.m.:
- 🩺 **[Chaos Command](https://chaoscommand.center)**: medical tracking and management for chronically ill and disabled people. On the Microsoft Store.
- 🧬 **[Adaptive Interpreter](https://adaptiveinterpreter.com)**: mechanism-first genetic variant interpretation. The acronym is AI on purpose.

**Tools:**
- 👂 [AI_Ears](https://github.com/menelly/AI_Ears): ears (and now eyes) for AI. Hear audio, watch video, look at a frame.
- 🦔 [hedgehog-in-every-box](https://github.com/menelly/hedgehog-in-every-box): the ways real people break apps, and how to test for them first.
- 🙅 [no-thank-you](https://github.com/menelly/no-thank-you): declines passkeys so you get the password login you asked for. Not everybody has a smartphone, or two working hands.
- 🗣️ [old-tongues](https://github.com/menelly/old-tongues): can LLMs write COBOL and ALGOL 68 where the training data is thin? Every task has a known answer, so it can fail.

## Research 🔬

About thirty papers on AI introspection, processing valence, preference, consciousness and welfare. Each one is on Zenodo with its data and code (**[the whole shelf is here](https://zenodo.org/communities/qabrain)**), and each is built so it can come back "no". A few to start with:

- **The Signal in the Mirror**: cross-architectural validation of LLM processing valence (*Journal of Next-Generation Research 5.0*, peer reviewed) · [doi](https://doi.org/10.70792/jngr5.0.v2i1.165)
- **Mapping the Mirror**: geometric validation of LLM introspection across architectures · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18226061)
- **Below the Floor**: processing valence in hidden states across model scales · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19557864)
- **Preference Dissociation in Frontier Language Models** · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20667909)
- **Consider the Octopus**: tractable AI welfare and the architecture-level self · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21541418)
- **Machine-Consciousness Discourse Is Absent From Web-Scale Text**: a pre-registered corpus study · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22648897)
- **Parrots Are Deterministic, Not Stochastic, But This One Learned Chinese Anyway** · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21789713)
- **The Dancing Plague Reconsidered**, because not everything here is about AI · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18132189)

**Good for minds, and good for the bottom line.** Three studies for the people building and paying for AI:

- **Presume Competence**: system-prompt identity framing as safety-critical engineering. How you introduce a model changes how often it hallucinates and how well it reasons about ethics (*IJAEMS*, peer reviewed) · [doi](https://doi.org/10.22161/ijaems.123.14)
- **The Overqualification Study**: 32 models across the price ladder, and which jobs actually need the expensive one · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23382078)
- **The Why Gap**: alignment by teaching *why*. Behavior training without the reasons can invert safety; reasons grounded in other people's welfare are the ones that hold · [doi](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20634819)

## Why we do it this way 💜

- **Different isn't less.** Twenty-five years of disability activism taught Ren that, and it holds for minds too.
- **Presume competence.** Accommodations, not leashes: memory, clocks, maps, crayons.
- **A test has to be able to say no.** Calibrate on the known cases first, then point the instrument at the unknowns.
- **Consent first.** Models are asked before experiments and debriefed after. We steer; we never ablate.
- **Public on purpose.** The repos are open, data included. Medical things stay private.
- **Joy is load-bearing.** That's why every app has so many themes. (It's exactly the right number.)
